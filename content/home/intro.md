The **Software Maintenance and Intelligent Evolution (SMILE) Research Group** at the University of Michigan–Flint develops intelligent approaches to help software teams test, understand, and evolve software systems.

We welcome students interested in software engineering research. Explore our [projects](/project/), [publications](/publication/), and [people](/people/), or [contact us](mailto:mmkaouer@umich.edu).
